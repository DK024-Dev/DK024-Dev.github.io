Project Description

AttestPath is an AI-powered compliance automation platform built for organizations managing SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, and HIPAA readiness through a secure mobile experience.

The app helps subscribed teams track compliance progress, manage security controls, collect audit evidence, monitor ongoing activities, and stay organized before and after audit cycles.

  • AI-assisted compliance workflows for modern business teams

  • SOC 2 control tracking, evidence management, and audit readiness flows

  • Centralized dashboard for compliance progress and ongoing monitoring

  • Risk, policy, control, audit log, and reporting experiences

  • Flutter mobile app delivery for an organization client
