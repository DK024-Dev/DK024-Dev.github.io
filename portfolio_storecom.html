Project Description

Store.com is a Flutter shopping discovery app built to keep users updated on daily deals, limited product drops, and selected offers without overwhelming the browsing experience.

The app focuses on a straightforward shopping flow, relevant recommendations, and clear product information so users can quickly decide what is worth their time.

  • Daily deal and limited-drop browsing experiences

  • Relevant recommendation flows based on user activity

  • Clean product details, availability, and offer screens

  • Flutter-based iOS, iPadOS, Android, and Apple silicon Mac support

  • Organization client delivery through AdMedia Digital Techno Lab
