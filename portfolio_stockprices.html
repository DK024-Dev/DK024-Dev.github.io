Project Description

StockPrices.com is a finance app that brings real-time stock prices, portfolio tracking, stock comparison, market news, and learning resources together in one mobile experience.

The app is designed to support users moving from basic stock tracking toward deeper analysis with clearer context behind price movement and market activity.

  • Real-time stock price tracking and market overview flows

  • Portfolio tracking and stock comparison experiences

  • Market news and learning resources for investor education

  • Upgrade paths for advanced tools and deeper insights

  • Flutter mobile app delivery for an organization client
