Project Description

Search.com is a Flutter-built AI search and shopping application designed to make search, discovery, image generation, and product research feel fast across mobile and desktop surfaces.

The portfolio entry includes the iOS/iPad app and the Search.com Shopping AI macOS app as the same product family, delivered for an organization client through AdMedia Digital Techno Lab.

  • Conversational AI search and shopping flows

  • Image preview, sharing, and download capabilities

  • Flutter mobile implementation for iOS, iPadOS, and Android

  • Flutter macOS desktop implementation for Shopping AI

  • UI improvements, performance updates, and feature enhancement cycles
