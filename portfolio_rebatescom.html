Project Description

Rebates.com is a Flutter savings app that brings coupons, cashback offers, and store discovery into one mobile experience. It is designed for shoppers who want to find savings quickly while browsing supported online stores.

The project also included iterative UI updates, performance improvements, and a Safari extension release cycle for the broader product experience.

  • Cashback and coupon browsing across online stores

  • Store discovery, offer details, and savings-focused flows

  • Account and contact flows connected to the shopping journey

  • Flutter implementation for iOS, iPadOS, Android, and Apple silicon Mac support

  • Continuous UI and performance update cycles
