Project Description

Claud.com is a Flutter AI product research app that helps users type a product name, discover relevant matches, compare options, and make faster shopping decisions without switching across multiple websites.

The experience combines AI-powered product recommendations with savings tracking, joining bonus, cashback, and product discovery flows for modern shopping habits.

  • AI-powered product search and recommendation flows

  • Product name search for instant research and matching

  • Multi-store comparison and better deal discovery

  • Cashback, joining bonus, and savings tracking experiences

  • Flutter mobile app delivery for an organization client
